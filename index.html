Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Moonlit Ninja respects your privacy.

1. Data Collection

Moonlit Ninja does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal information or sensitive user data. The game does not require account registration or login.

2. Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, records, and unlock information may be stored locally on your device. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

3. Advertising

The current version of Moonlit Ninja does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs. If advertising is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated accordingly.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

Moonlit Ninja does not sell, share, or transfer personal user data to third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

Moonlit Ninja does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Moonlit Ninja does not collect or store personal information on external servers, the developer does not retain personal user data. Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the application.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game’s features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact fggames.support@gmail.com.
